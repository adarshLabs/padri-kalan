## Deliberately left out

- Names of the current Pradhan, MLA and MP. Directories show names that may be out of date after elections, and the site stays out of politics. Add them later only from an official source, if the village wants.
- geoiq.io's "2020 population 7,434" is a model estimate, not a census figure.
- Exact map coordinates, because no reliable source was found. The site links to a map search instead.
